Objective.The study will combine incomplete groundwater records, environmental drivers, and
spatial relationships to forecast future levels, reconstruct missing periods, and predict at locations
without groundwater histories. The research gap will be addressed through an inductive spatiotemporal
model, explicit missing-input uncertainty, and spatially blocked evaluation.
A. Overall System Architecture
Inputswill include groundwater measurements and timestamps, well coordinates, meteorological
histories, and verified static attributes.Processingwill include quality control, monthly aggregation,
spatial matching, missing-data reconstruction, temporal encoding, graph message passing, and uncer-
tainty calibration.Outputswill be groundwater-depth estimates, nominal 90% prediction intervals,
reconstructed observations, and spatial error summaries.

B. Dataset Description
Source and study period.The intended period will be January 2021–December 2025. Groundwater
records will be obtained from Karnataka telemetry resources on the National Water Data Portal
(NWDP), maintained by the National Water Informatics Centre. The portal identifies 2021–2025
groundwater resources and Karnataka rainfall listings [1]. Actual record coverage, cadence, coordinates,
units, and measurement references will be audited before modelling.
T arget and sample unit.The intended target will be groundwater depth in metres below ground
level, after verifying the source definition. Smaller depth will indicate shallower groundwater; this
quantity will not be interpreted directly as hydraulic head. Each modelling row will represent one
canonical well and month. Source networks will be deduplicated only when physical well identity is
verified.
Number of samples.The raw datasets have not yet been ingested, so empirical counts are pending.
The data inventory will report unique wells, raw measured records, valid observed well–month targets,
missingness, and usable windows in each partition. WithN wells and 60 calendar months,60N is the
possible grid size, not the measured sample count. A complete series with lookbackL and horizonh
will provide at most60−L−h + 1windows: 48 for L = 12and h = 1. Imputed targets will not be
counted as measured samples.

Preprocessing will proceed as follows:
1. Audit identifiers, coordinates, timestamps, units, duplicates, sensor errors, and measurement-
reference changes. Flag suspicious values; exclude them only with a documented reason.
2. Convert timestamps consistently to Asia/Kolkata. Reduce telemetry to daily medians, then monthly
medians. Require at least ten valid observation days per telemetry month; other targets will
remain missing. Sparse seasonal measurements will not become measured monthly labels through
interpolation.
3. Aggregate rainfall using verified incremental/cumulative definitions and retain monthly coverage.
Map meteorological stations to well coordinates using up to five available neighbours and inverse-
distance weighting with power two. Use geodesic distances; join station IDs directly only for verified
shared installations.
4. Preserve original target-observation masks and separate input-hiding masks. Fit scaling, imputers,
feature selection, and empirical thresholds within each training fold. Use global training-target
scaling, since query wells have no history for per-well scaling.
5. Fill missing environmental inputs with their latest known value for at most one month, then a
training-only calendar-month median, always retaining availability flags. Missing static values will
use training medians or an unknown category.
The core training cohort will require at least 70% valid monthly targets during 2021–2023. Other
training wells will support a separate sparse-data analysis. Test wells will be scored wherever genuine
observations exist.
C. Proposed Model / Algorithm
Feature engineering.Historical groundwater, input masks, rainfall and temperature sequences,
trailing three- and six-month rainfall totals, and seasonal sine/cosine encodings will form the initial
dynamic inputs. Coordinates and verified static attributes will supply spatial context. Lagged
relationships and validation-based feature-group ablation will guide selection; weak instantaneous
correlation alone will not justify removal.
Temporal and spatial learning.A GRU will encode a six- or twelve-month history, followed by
two GATv2 layers and an MLP output head. Graph learning is motivated by multi-well studies in the
submitted survey [2], while GATv2 supports query-dependent attention [3]. Letyi,t denote depth,ri,t

Imputed targets will never receive an observed-target mask. The conditional Gaussian assumption will

Prediction without target-well history .A query node will receive environmental history, static
attributes, and an all-missing groundwater mask, with zero placeholders on the standardized scale. It
will receive neighbour messages but will not provide messages to other nodes. No well-specific learned
ID embedding will be used. During training, 20% of target wells per batch will act as pseudo-queries:
their complete groundwater input histories and derived features will be hidden before imputation and
graph construction, while observed future targets remain available for the loss. This will teach the
model the inference condition. The task is stricter than excluding a well from training while providing
its groundwater history at inference, as in Zhuang et al. [4].
Missing-data uncertainty .DeepMVI will be fitted on training records using artificial isolated and
block masks [5]. Its conditional Gaussian sampling model will use residual variance estimated from an
internal 10% masked-training subset. For forecasting, reconstruction inputs will be truncated at each
origin; retrospective reconstruction may use surrounding observations. Entirely unmonitored query
histories will not be imputed into groundwater features.

D. Training & Testing Strategy
Spatial split.Wells will be grouped into 25-km cells using EPSG:32643 coordinates and cell indices
(⌊x/25000⌋,⌊y/ 25000⌋). Occupied cells will be sorted and shuffled with seed 42. Whole cells will
be assigned sequentially to approximately 20% test wells, then 16% spatial-validation wells, leaving
approximately 64% training wells. Actual assignments will be recorded. Validation/test wells will enter
only as query nodes, with all groundwater histories hidden. Geodesic distances will define graph edges
and station matching.
Validation and tuning.Rolling-origin development checks will fit through December 2022 and
validate January–June 2023, then fit through June 2023 and validate July–December 2023. Every
learned transformation will be refitted inside its fold. Twelve configurations will be sampled using

and test observations will not select features or parameters. Test forecasts will begin after calibration,
using newly available histories without updating model weights.
Experiments and comparisons.
1. Forecast monitored wells against persistence, seasonal naive, Ridge, Random Forest, XGBoost, and
a GRU without graph learning.
2. Test no-history wells against environmental/static regressors and neighbour-persistence forecasts.
IDW using target-date neighbour observations will be reported separately as contemporaneous
spatial estimation.
3. Hide 10%, 20%, 30%, and 40% of observed values using isolated and one- to three-month block
masks, with five realizations each. Score reconstruction only at hidden measured values; assess
forecast degradation separately with scoring targets intact.
4. Remove graph learning, static attributes, pseudo-query training, or imputation sampling one at a
time; test optional feature groups using the same partitions and comparable fitting budgets.
Source versions, quality-control rules, fold manifests, features, configurations, seeds, software versions,
and predictions will be saved. Checks will confirm that query-well groundwater and post-origin
observations never enter forecast inputs.
